The Kids

Five Robotics Graduands and a dream, It doesn't get better than that.

We are a fashion-tech startup founded by five robotics graduands from the Federal University of Technology, Owerri (FUTO). What began as an idea — the brainchild of Ikechukwu — quickly evolved into a shared vision embraced by all five of us: Chibueze, Wilfrid, Tochi, Perfect, and Ikechukwu.
Offmark, we believe that fashion should be as intelligent as the people who wear it. Drawing from our background in robotics, we build clothing and wearable systems that integrate functionality, automation, and data — without compromising style.
